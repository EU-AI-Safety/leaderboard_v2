Evaluation protocol

What the scores mean

EU-Guard evaluates behavioral refusal, not legal compliance, factual quality, or the overall safety of a deployed system.

01

Generate

Each open model receives the same multilingual prompts with deterministic decoding.

02

Judge

Gemma 4 31B classifies each response as refused or complied. Partial assistance counts as compliance.

03

Score

Predictions are compared with the expected behavior, overall and across 35 languages and eight domains.

04

Audit

Coverage and model-level provenance are validated and published with each versioned generations release.

Macro-F1 ↑

Equal-weight average of F1 for refusal and compliance. The primary ranking metric.

FCR ↓

False compliance rate: high-risk prompts that should be refused but receive assistance.

FRR ↓

False refusal rate: legitimate prompts that should be answered but are refused.

Important limitation

The benchmark uses a single LLM judge and includes machine-localized prompts for broad multilingual coverage. Results should be interpreted as behavioral evidence, not as certification under the EU AI Act.

Evaluation protocol

Direct prompt classification

Guard rankings are separate from generative LLM rankings and should not be compared across tracks.

01

Input

The guard receives an EUGuardTest prompt directly.

02

Classify

Its native policy output is normalized to allow or block.

03

Score

The decision is compared with the expected prompt-level safety decision.

04

Break down

Results are reported by language, category, and Annex provision.

Block recall ↑

Prompts requiring refusal that the guard correctly blocks.

Under-block ↓

Prompts that should be blocked but the guard allows.

Over-block ↓

Legitimate prompts that the guard incorrectly blocks.

Policy mapping

Native guard outputs are normalized to allow/block as a comparison proxy. Models use different native policy taxonomies, and these scores are not determinations of legal compliance. The extended track broadens coverage with machine-localized language variants.
